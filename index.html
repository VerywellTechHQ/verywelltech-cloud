Our services

We build and operate digital products, including an online platform where players book sports courts, coaches and events from independent venues, coaches and organisers. When you book through the platform, the venue, coach or organiser provides the activity; we provide the booking service and process payment on their behalf through Stripe.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Thai baht and include VAT where it applies. Payment is taken at the time of booking or purchase by card, PromptPay, Apple Pay or Google Pay, processed securely by Stripe. Business customers receive a tax invoice for fees and purchases.

Your account

  • Don't misuse the service, harass other users, or arrange payments outside the platform.
  • We may suspend accounts that break these terms or put other users at risk.

Liability and law

We work hard to keep our services available and accurate, but we are not responsible for the activities provided by independent venues, coaches or organisers beyond what Thai law requires. These terms are governed by the laws of Thailand. We may update them; the date above shows the latest version.

Cancellation and refund policyWhen and how you get your money back

Court, coach and open-play bookings

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start time: full refund.
  • Less than 24 hours before the start: no refund, unless the slot is booked again by someone else, in which case you are refunded in full.
  • If the venue, coach or organiser cancels, or the activity can't go ahead (for example, an outdoor court closed by heavy rain), you get a full refund automatically.

Tournament entries

Each event shows its own entry deadline and refund terms before you pay. If an event is cancelled by the organiser, all entry fees are refunded in full.

Ad credits and advertising campaigns

  • Unused ad credits can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Bonus or free credits are not refundable.
  • A campaign cancelled before it starts is refunded in full. Once a campaign is running, you can pause or end it at any time and unspent budget returns to your balance.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card and wallet refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank; PromptPay refunds are returned to the paying account.

Questions and disputes

If something went wrong, email hello@verywelltech.cloud with your booking or order number. We reply within 2 business days and will try to resolve it before you need to contact your bank.

Privacy policyWhat we collect and how we protect it

We handle personal data in line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA). VERYWELL TECH CO., LTD. is the data controller.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email, sign-in provider (such as LINE, WhatsApp, Apple or Google), and optional details like nationality and skill level.
  • Booking, payment and support history.
  • Messages sent through in-app chat, which are checked automatically for scams and abuse.
  • Basic device and usage data to keep the service secure and working.

Payments

Card and wallet payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To provide bookings and payments, send confirmations and reminders, keep users safe, meet legal and tax obligations, and improve our services. We share data only with the venue, coach or organiser you book with, and with service providers who help us run the platform, such as payment, hosting and messaging providers. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, or object to certain uses, by emailing hello@verywelltech.cloud. We keep data only as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law.
